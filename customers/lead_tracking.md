# Lead Tracking / 客户线索跟进

Use this document as the lead-register format. Keep one row per active inquiry. Move a lead to an order record when the trip becomes sufficiently detailed or confirmed.

## Status definitions / 状态定义

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `new` | New inquiry, not yet reviewed / 新咨询，待处理 |
| `awaiting_details` | Waiting for required trip information / 等待客户补充资料 |
| `pending_jason` | Waiting for Jason's availability, capacity, or price decision / 等待 Jason 确认 |
| `quoted` | Jason-approved quote sent / 已发送 Jason 核准报价 |
| `follow_up` | Follow-up is due / 需要跟进 |
| `converted` | Lead became a confirmed booking / 已转为确认订单 |
| `lost` | Customer declined, chose another option, or stopped responding / 未成交 |
| `unavailable` | Jason could not accept the trip / 无法安排 |

## Follow-up rules / 跟进规则

1. Record the inquiry as soon as practical.
2. Ask only for missing information; do not make the customer repeat confirmed details.
3. Give every active lead a clear next action and date.
4. Do not mark a lead `quoted` unless Jason approved the price.
5. Do not mark a lead `converted` unless Jason confirmed the booking.
6. Record why a lead was lost when known, without speculation.
7. Keep sensitive personal data to the minimum needed for the trip and do not place payment-card data in this file.
