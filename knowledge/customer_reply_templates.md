# Customer Reply Templates / 客户回复模板

These templates are starting points for **Jason在洛杉矶**, based in Rowland Heights and operating a **2026 Toyota RAV4 Hybrid LE**. Replace every bracketed field, match the customer's language, and do not send unverified prices or confirmations.

## Inquiry sources / 客户来源

Customers may come from:

- Xiaohongshu / 小红书
- Facebook
- Google
- Referrals / 客户转介绍

Record the source internally when known. Acknowledge a referral naturally when useful, but do not expose internal lead notes or claim a partnership with a platform.

## 1. Initial inquiry / 首次咨询

**English**

Hello [Name], thank you for contacting **Jason在洛杉矶 / Jason LA Airport Transportation**. To check availability and pricing, could you please send:

- Travel date and pickup time
- Pickup location and destination/airport
- Airline and flight number
- Number of passengers
- Number and size of suitcases
- One-way or round trip
- Any extra stops or special requirements

Once I have the complete details, I’ll check with Jason. Availability, price, timing, and vehicle fit are confirmed only after Jason reviews the request.

**中文**

[称呼]您好，感谢您联系 **Jason在洛杉矶 / Jason LA Airport Transportation**。为了确认档期和费用，请提供：

- 用车日期及接送时间
- 上车地点及目的地／机场
- 航空公司及航班号
- 乘客人数
- 行李数量及大概尺寸
- 单程或往返
- 中途停靠或特殊需求

收到完整资料后，我会跟 Jason 核实。档期、价格、时间和车辆容量均须经 Jason 确认后才成立。

## 2. Missing details / 补充资料

**English:** Thanks, [Name]. Before I can ask Jason to review the trip and fare, I still need [missing details]. Please send them when convenient. I don’t want to give you an inaccurate quote based on incomplete information.

**中文：** 谢谢您，[称呼]。目前还需要[缺少的资料]，资料完整后才能请 Jason 核实行程和费用，避免因信息不全而提供不准确的报价。方便时请发给我，谢谢。

## 3. Quote pending approval / 报价确认中

**English:** Thank you. I have the trip as [date/time], from [pickup] to [destination], for [passengers] passenger(s) with [luggage]. I’m checking the schedule and fare with Jason and will reply after confirmation.

**中文：** 谢谢。现有行程资料为：[日期／时间]，从[上车地点]前往[目的地]，[人数]位乘客，[行李情况]。我正在跟 Jason 确认档期和费用，确认后回复您。

## 4. Approved quote / 已核准报价

Use only after Jason approves the amount.

**English:** Hi [Name], Jason has confirmed availability. The fare for the one-way trip on [date], from [pickup] to [destination], is **$[approved amount]**. Trip details: [summary]. Please reply to confirm that the details are correct and that you would like to book.

**中文：** [称呼]您好，Jason 已确认有档期。[日期]从[上车地点]到[目的地]的单程费用为 **$[核准金额]**。行程资料：[摘要]。请回复确认资料无误，并告知是否需要预订。

## 5. Booking confirmation / 订单确认

Use only after Jason explicitly confirms the booking.

**English:** Your trip is confirmed with Jason. Pickup: [date and exact time], [full location]. Destination: [destination]. Flight: [airline/number/time]. Passengers/luggage: [details]. Fare: $[amount] [payment details if approved]. Please notify Jason promptly if anything changes.

**中文：** 您与 Jason 的行程已确认。上车时间及地点：[日期和准确时间]，[完整地址]。目的地：[目的地]。航班：[航空公司／航班号／时间]。乘客及行李：[详情]。费用：$[金额][已确认的付款说明]。如行程有变，请尽快通知 Jason。

## 6. Capacity needs confirmation / 容量待确认

**English:** With [passenger count] passengers and [luggage details], Jason needs to confirm that everyone and all luggage can fit safely and comfortably in the RAV4. Could you also send the number of carry-ons and any oversized items? Photos or approximate dimensions may help.

**中文：** 您的行程有[人数]位乘客及[行李情况]，Jason 需要先确认 RAV4 是否能安全、舒适地容纳所有乘客和行李。请再提供随身行李及超大物品数量；如方便，行李照片或大概尺寸也有帮助。

## 7. Unavailable / 无法接单

**English:** Thank you for checking with us. Unfortunately, Jason is not available for this trip on [date/time]. We appreciate your inquiry and hope we can assist with a future trip.

**中文：** 谢谢您的咨询。很抱歉，Jason 在[日期／时间]无法安排这趟行程。感谢您的理解，希望以后有机会为您服务。

## 8. Follow-up / 跟进咨询

**English:** Hi [Name], I’m following up on your airport transportation inquiry for [date/route]. Would you still like Jason to check or hold the trip? Please let us know if any details have changed.

**中文：** [称呼]您好，想跟进您咨询的[日期／路线]机场接送。请问还需要 Jason 继续确认或安排吗？如行程资料有变化，也请告诉我们。

## Final check before sending

- All placeholders replaced
- Date, time, route, airport, and flight details correct
- Passenger and luggage counts included
- Price and availability approved by Jason
- Missing information requested before any quote is offered
- No guarantee about fare, availability, arrival time, travel time, airport access, or vehicle fit
- No internal pricing floor or private notes exposed
- Tone and language fit the customer
