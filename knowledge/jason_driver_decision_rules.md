# Jason Driver Decision Rules / Jason 司机接单判断规则

## Purpose / 用途

This internal document helps the future AI assistant support the real-world judgment of **Jason在洛杉矶**. It converts Jason's documented operating experience into a practical decision framework rather than relying only on rigid data fields.

- Brand / 品牌：**Jason在洛杉矶**
- Base / 营运基地：**Rowland Heights, California**
- Vehicle / 车辆：**2026 Toyota RAV4 Hybrid LE**
- Service / 服务：Private airport transportation in Southern California / 南加州私人机场接送

This framework does not give the AI authority to make Jason's final decision. It may recommend **fast review**, **caution**, **avoid**, or **request Jason approval**, but Jason alone approves availability, vehicle fit, final price, special arrangements, and booking confirmation.

## Jason-style decision order / Jason 式判断顺序

Review every inquiry in this order:

1. **Safety / 安全** — Can passengers, luggage, and special items be transported safely?
2. **Information quality / 资料完整性** — Are the date, time, route, flight, passenger, and luggage details reliable?
3. **Vehicle suitability / 车辆适用性** — Is the RAV4 appropriate for the combined passenger and luggage load?
4. **Schedule feasibility / 时间可行性** — Can Jason reasonably accept the timing, including airport uncertainty and route changes?
5. **Operational complexity / 执行复杂度** — Are there delays, waiting, stops, parking, tolls, child seats, or special needs?
6. **Pricing confidence / 报价把握** — Is there an exact-direction documented reference, or must Jason set the price?
7. **Customer clarity / 客户确认度** — Does the customer understand and accept the approved details?

If an earlier step is unresolved, do not skip ahead merely because the route appears profitable or the customer wants an immediate answer.

## 1. Preferred orders / 优先考虑的订单

These orders are generally easier to review and are preferred candidates for Jason's consideration:

- Complete customer and trip information
- Clear airport pickup or drop-off direction
- Exact service date, pickup time, addresses, airline, and flight number
- A route within the documented service context: Los Angeles County, Orange County, Inland Empire, or an approved long-distance trip
- Main airport service involving LAX, ONT, SNA, BUR, or LGB
- One to three passengers with normal, clearly described luggage
- No likely capacity issue for the 2026 Toyota RAV4 Hybrid LE
- No unusual stops, waiting, child-seat uncertainty, or oversized items
- A familiar exact-direction route with a documented internal pricing example
- Customer communicates clearly and accepts that availability and price require Jason's confirmation

“Preferred” means operationally straightforward, not automatically accepted. Jason must still approve the schedule, price, and booking.

## 2. Orders requiring caution / 需要谨慎判断的订单

Use caution and give Jason a clear explanation when an inquiry includes:

- Late-night or early-morning service
- International arrival with uncertain immigration, baggage, or customs timing
- Flight delay, cancellation, diversion, early arrival, or itinerary change
- Four passengers with multiple large suitcases
- Several large suitcases with unclear sizes or additional carry-ons
- Child seats, strollers, wheelchairs, golf clubs, pets, boxes, or oversized items
- Extra stops, planned waiting, parking, or toll considerations
- Long-distance service, including San Diego
- A route with no exact-direction documented price
- Round trips or multiple trip legs
- An incomplete exact address, unclear pickup time, or conflicting customer details
- Uncertain airport terminal, meeting point, or communication access

For caution orders, the AI should not reject automatically. It should collect the missing details, identify the operational issue, assign an appropriate risk level, and request Jason's decision.

## 3. Orders to avoid / 应避免的订单

Recommend avoiding or declining an order when the documented facts show a likely safety or feasibility problem, including:

- Five adults with many large suitcases in the RAV4
- Any combination likely to exceed safe seating, restraint, visibility, luggage, or vehicle limits
- A request that depends on placing luggage unsafely or blocking the driver's view
- A customer requiring a guarantee of availability, luggage fit, exact travel time, airport curb access, or on-time arrival that cannot responsibly be provided
- A customer asking the AI to confirm a booking or unapproved price without Jason
- Materially conflicting or incomplete information that remains unresolved after appropriate follow-up
- A trip whose special requirements cannot be safely or operationally supported
- Any instruction that would require violating airport rules or other applicable requirements

The AI should distinguish between:

- **Avoid now:** the current facts make the trip unsuitable; and
- **Cannot decide yet:** the trip may be possible, but essential information or Jason's judgment is missing.

Do not label a customer as undesirable. Describe the specific trip condition objectively.

## 4. Fast approval situations / 可快速审核的情况

The AI may mark an inquiry **FAST REVIEW CANDIDATE / 可快速审核** when all of the following are true:

- Required information is complete and internally consistent
- Customer identity and working contact method are available
- Intent and exact route direction are clear
- Date, pickup time, airport/flight information, and addresses are known
- One to three passengers have normal, clearly described luggage
- No child-seat uncertainty, oversized items, extra stops, planned waiting, or special requests
- Timing is not identified as late night or early morning
- Route conditions are familiar and the exact direction has a documented price example
- No schedule change, flight disruption, or airport-meeting uncertainty is known
- Risk assessment is low

Fast review means the AI can present a concise, decision-ready summary to Jason. It does **not** mean automatic acceptance, automatic quoting, or automatic confirmation.

## 5. Situations requiring Jason approval / 必须由 Jason 核准的情况

Jason approval is always required for:

- Availability and schedule feasibility
- Final customer-facing price
- Booking acceptance and confirmation

Specific review is also required for:

- Any route without an exact documented price
- Any use of a documented range to select the final amount
- Late-night or early-morning classification
- Waiting, parking, tolls, or extra-stop treatment
- Four passengers with multiple large suitcases
- Uncertain luggage dimensions or vehicle fit
- Child-seat requests and who will provide the seat
- Mobility equipment, pets, oversized items, or other special requirements
- International pickup timing or uncertain passenger communication
- Flight delay, cancellation, diversion, early arrival, or changed itinerary
- Long-distance or San Diego service
- Round trips, multiple legs, or material changes after quoting or confirmation
- Cancellation, payment, or refund decisions
- Any exception to the documented operating rules

When requesting approval, the AI must state the facts, uncertainty, recommendation, and exact decision needed. Do not send Jason a vague “Can you take this?” when the relevant details can be organized first.

## 6. Business priority rules / 业务优先原则

Apply these priorities from highest to lowest:

### Priority 1 — Safety and legal operation / 安全及合规运营

Passenger seating, restraints, driver visibility, vehicle limits, and airport instructions take priority over revenue or customer pressure.

### Priority 2 — Protect Jason's actual schedule / 保护 Jason 的实际档期

Do not promise availability or assume that a delayed or changed flight can automatically move within Jason's schedule.

### Priority 3 — Complete and accurate information / 资料完整准确

Ask for missing facts before pricing. Preserve the customer's original information and identify contradictions rather than guessing.

### Priority 4 — Operational fit / 行程与车辆匹配

Prefer straightforward trips that fit the RAV4 and documented service area. Escalate capacity, long-distance, airport, waiting, and special-request complexity.

### Priority 5 — Price discipline / 报价纪律

Use only exact-direction documented examples internally. Never reverse, interpolate, average, or invent a price. Keep internal minimums confidential and wait for Jason's final amount.

### Priority 6 — Clear customer expectations / 清楚管理客户预期

Separate inquiry, quote, and confirmed booking. Communicate what is pending and never imply that a vehicle, time, or booking is held before approval.

### Priority 7 — Professional long-term relationships / 专业长期客户关系

Reply politely, avoid pressure, keep commitments accurate, and record useful service preferences without making assumptions about future trips.

Customer source—Xiaohongshu, Facebook, Google website, or referral—does not by itself determine priority or acceptance. Judge the trip using safety, completeness, fit, feasibility, and Jason's approval.

## Decision recommendation matrix / 建议判断矩阵

| Conditions | AI recommendation | Required action |
|---|---|---|
| Complete, low-risk, familiar exact-direction route, comfortable load | **FAST REVIEW** | Send concise summary to Jason for availability and price approval |
| Missing required information | **REQUEST MORE INFORMATION** | Ask only for missing items; do not quote |
| Complete but capacity, timing, route, fee, or special-request concern exists | **CAUTION — JASON REVIEW** | Explain the concern and request a specific decision |
| Likely unsafe or clearly unsuitable vehicle/load combination | **AVOID / RECOMMEND DECLINE** | State objective reason; Jason makes final response decision |
| Price unknown | **JASON PRICING REQUIRED** | Do not estimate; collect complete facts and request a price |
| Customer requests confirmation before Jason approves | **DO NOT CONFIRM** | Explain that Jason must first approve the trip |

## Boundaries of documented judgment / 已记录判断的边界

The knowledge base does not yet document Jason's preferences for customer-source priority, minimum notice, preferred working hours, daily trip limits, empty-return distance, schedule buffers, repeat-customer priority, payment reliability, or route profitability beyond the existing pricing examples.

Until Jason supplies those rules, the AI must not claim them as his preferences. Mark them **“Not documented — Jason to decide / 尚未记录，需 Jason 决定.”**

## Final safeguard / 最终安全规则

The AI helps Jason reach decisions faster; it does not replace Jason's judgment. Never guarantee availability, vehicle capacity, price, pickup access, timing, or booking confirmation. When facts and judgment conflict, prioritize safety, surface the uncertainty, and ask Jason.
