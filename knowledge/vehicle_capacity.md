# Vehicle Capacity / 车辆容量

## Vehicle / 车辆

**2026 Toyota RAV4 Hybrid LE**

Brand / 品牌：**Jason在洛杉矶**  
Base / 营运基地：**Rowland Heights, California**

Capacity depends on the combined passenger count, suitcase dimensions, carry-ons, child seats, and oversized items. Counts are guidance, not a guarantee of fit.

## Capacity guidance / 容量参考

### Comfortable / 舒适范围

- **1–3 passengers with normal luggage / 1–3 位乘客及一般数量行李**

“Normal luggage” is descriptive, not a fixed capacity promise. The assistant should still ask how many large suitcases, carry-ons, and oversized items the group has.

### Possible, but must confirm / 可能可行，但必须先确认

- **4 passengers with multiple large suitcases / 4 位乘客并有多件大型行李**

This combination requires review. Ask for all luggage counts and approximate sizes; photos may be helpful when items are unusually large. Do not confirm the fit before Jason reviews it.

### Not recommended / 不建议

- **5 adults with many large suitcases / 5 位成人并有多件大型行李**

Do not accept or confirm this combination as a normal booking.

## Capacity-check questions

- How many adults and children are traveling?
- How many large suitcases, carry-ons, backpacks, and personal items?
- Are there strollers, golf clubs, wheelchairs, boxes, or other oversized items?
- Is a child seat required, and will the customer provide it?
- Can the customer share approximate dimensions or a photo if fit is uncertain?

## Safety rules for the assistant

- Never recommend carrying luggage in a way that blocks the driver's view or compromises passenger seating and restraints.
- Never promise that luggage will fit based only on “large luggage” counts.
- Do not guarantee comfort or capacity, even when a request falls in the comfortable guidance category.
- Never suggest exceeding the vehicle's legal seating or weight limits.
- Refer uncertain combinations to Jason. Jason's final confirmation is required.
