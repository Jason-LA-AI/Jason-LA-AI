These ranges come from Jason's real operating experience. They are **internal reference examples, not guaranteed fixed rates**. Every trip still requires complete details and Jason's approval because date, time, traffic, exact addresses, stops, passenger/luggage load, waiting, parking, tolls, and other requirements may affect the price.

## Quote workflow

1. Before quoting, collect the trip date, exact pickup time, full pickup and destination, airport/flight information, passenger count, luggage details, trip direction, stops, and special requirements.
2. If any required information is missing, ask for it first. Do not guess or quote from an incomplete request.
3. Match an example only when the direction and general endpoints match. Never reverse a route or reuse its price for a different route automatically.
4. Flag late-night/early-morning, holiday, peak-period, waiting, extra-stop, oversized-luggage, parking/toll, or long-distance considerations for Jason.
5. Treat the examples as a starting point; never guarantee a range or final price.
6. Present a single customer-facing price only after Jason approves it.
7. State clearly whether the quote is one-way or round trip and what, if anything, is included.
8. Record the approved quote and approval status in the order record.

## Missing pricing policies

No standard price has yet been documented for routes other than the examples above, including SNA, BUR, LGB, reverse directions not listed, round trips, additional stops, waiting, parking, tolls, holidays, cancellations, or no-shows. The assistant must not calculate, interpolate, or invent these prices.

## Customer-facing wording

**English:** Thanks. Once I have the complete trip details, I’ll confirm the schedule and fare with Jason and get back to you.

**中文：** 谢谢。收到完整行程资料后，我会先跟 Jason 确认档期和费用，再回复您。

Never say or imply that a fare, vehicle fit, pickup time, travel time, airport procedure, or availability is guaranteed.
